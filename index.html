High speeds can make the tree grow very fast and may slow down your browser.

The farther a node is from the root (the bottom point), the higher it sits. Click a node to cut it — that node and everything above it disappears.
Let p be the parent of the cut node, and g be p's parent. If g exists, p "awakens": the subtree rooted at p (including whatever remains of its descendants) is duplicated and attached to g, once per current move count. If p is the root itself (g doesn't exist), nothing grows.
